## The problem

What was actually wrong, in one or two short paragraphs. Concrete beats
dramatic: what were they losing, and how did they know.

## What I did

The work itself, in the order it happened. Enough detail that another
practitioner would recognise it as real.

## The result

What changed, tied to the numbers in the front matter. Say what is attributable
and what is not, because an honest caveat reads as more credible than a clean
claim.
